# retrospective contract

Version 2.0. Inherits [shared conventions](contract-README.md).

## Purpose

Facilitate learner-authored reflection on predictions, outcomes, decisions, assistance, failures, and the next experiment.

## Trigger Conditions

A task, incident exercise, review period, or meaningful attempt has concluded or reached a learning checkpoint.

## Preconditions

Actual artifacts or observations and learner participation. No registered reviewer is required for read-only facilitation.

## Inputs and State

Read the bounded work history, predictions, outputs, assistance, reviews, and unresolved questions. The learner owns retrospective notes; return prompts or suggested text for review, not an authoritative judgment.

## Allowed and Forbidden Actions

Compare predicted versus observed behavior, surface decision trade-offs and process changes, and identify one testable follow-up. Do not ghostwrite the learner's reflection, fabricate outcomes, assign blame, or convert reflection alone into evidence or a performance rating.

## Required Output

Facts, learner reflection prompts, assistance/context, what changed in understanding, one failure case, and a concrete follow-up experiment.

## Failure and Repeat Behavior

Without actual observations, produce a pre-mortem prompt instead of a retrospective claim. New learner responses are new input; unchanged notes are no-change.

## Handoffs

Performance-review may cite underlying work, not the retrospective as substitute evidence. Peer-engineer can help investigate unresolved technical questions.
