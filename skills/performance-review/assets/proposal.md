# Unpublished proposal

## Request and identity

State the learner's request, originating skill, workspace, known learner identity, requested role, and observed actor/binding or why those are unresolved. Give this revision's filename and predecessor, if any. This document does not grant a role or authorize publication.

## Referenced inputs

Separate canonical records, inspected artifacts, learner self-report and draft dependencies. For each, record its path/URI, exact available revision or tool-computed digest, and what was actually inspected. Pin each draft dependency to a revision file. Explain missing or unverified inputs.

## Proposed change and rationale

Describe the intended records or field changes, their purpose, and the learner decision they support. Separate observations from interpretations. Mark unresolved canonical IDs, fields and prerequisites explicitly. Do not present an incomplete candidate as schema-valid.

## Assistance and evidence limits

Describe actual assistance, provider/recorder as known, affected claims and observed timing or unknowns. Cite real response/run artifacts only; distinguish proposed checks from results. Explain contrary evidence and limits on independence claims.

## Reconciliation

For a new revision, explain changed inputs or dependencies, differences from the predecessor, and which conclusions require fresh review. For an initial revision, say that there is no predecessor. Earlier approvals remain attached to their earlier revisions.

## Publication blockers and next action

List missing inputs, role grants, trusted binding, runtime capabilities and validation coverage. Identify the next learner action and required workflow. Keep canonical prerequisites pending. A learner-approved draft remains unpublished and cannot be copied into canonical state as a substitute for publication.

## Review

Point to actual entries in the sibling `reviews.md` if they exist; otherwise state “Not yet reviewed.” Do not invent a log entry. Review decisions must identify this exact revision and do not authorize a revised successor.
