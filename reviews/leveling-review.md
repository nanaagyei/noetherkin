# Leveling and educational-integrity review

Keep evidence-based adjacent promotions, all-core coverage, contrary evidence, and separate completion versus capability. There is no explicit XP, task quota or numeric competency average to remove.

## F07 · HIGH · Mandatory level dimensions impose a service-engineering specialty

**Confirmed issue.** [Leveling framework](../docs/architecture/leveling-framework.md) makes all thirteen dimensions mandatory. [Level catalog](../catalog/levels.yaml) E3 explicitly requires cross-service debugging, service contracts, migration controls and load/recovery across services; E4 scope requires services or infrastructure. The charter also targets GPU, research and ML engineers. Selecting different competencies does not change these mandatory expectations.

**Why it matters.** An irrelevant specialty can become a promotion barrier even though the competency model explicitly rejects universal specialization requirements.

**Failure scenario.** A learner delivers a substantial local inference kernel or offline ML evaluation improvement, with independent diagnosis, rigorous testing, compatibility and sustained outcomes. They must construct unrelated service exercises to satisfy literal E3 scope/debugging/collaboration wording, or a reviewer silently rewrites the rubric to pass them.

**Recommended correction.** Preserve the dimensions but make their normative behaviors domain-neutral, with versioned specialty examples showing equivalent scope, operation and collaboration. Alternatively declare V1's level rubric service-engineering-only and limit its advertised applicability. Do not allow unexplained “not applicable” on weak core skills. Validate at least one non-service progression example before claiming project-independent levels.

## F14 · MEDIUM · Simulated collaborators can manufacture apparent leadership effects

The framework permits simulated production/collaboration evidence and requires attributable E5 effects. It does not say what separates a learner-caused result from a model simply narrating agreement or improved practices. Role IDs and text artifacts alone are insufficient. Require observable changes against an independently specified exercise constraint, actual learner interactions and a counterfactual/failure probe. Distinguish simulated outcomes in the evidence context, not only export caveats. Calibration is Unknown until exercised. Do not claim real organizational experience or require fake teams for ordinary learning.

## F15 · MEDIUM · Independence judgment is under-calibrated for healthy help

The assistance policy correctly says help is not a global penalty and E1/E2 explicitly permit help at limits. Nevertheless, `independent` versus `guided` has no operational boundary for navigation hints or expert consultation. The sole fixture labels a documentation pointer guided/E0; it should not become the default rule for all level-1 assistance. Provide a few paired calibration examples: locating a README versus locating the exact faulty line; naming a concept versus giving the causal explanation; suggesting an experiment versus supplying its expected answer; reading an isolated example versus copying an isomorphic solution. Classify help by what it reveals about the claim, not by syntax or total maximum level. A reviewer must explain which decisions remained the learner's.

## Educational conclusions

Blind copying and solution leakage are explicitly discouraged in assignment, teach and peer contracts. Known copied code can still produce an honest completed task with no implementation competency. Passing code plus an inability to explain it should produce contrary evidence, not a guessed understanding score. Genuine debugging requires a tested causal account, not just a patch that works.

These are specification strengths, not measured learning efficacy. Deliberate undisclosed AI use remains outside the promised assurance level. Prefer a short new failure-case or transfer exercise over surveillance, forced isolation, repetitive quizzes, or mandatory corporate roleplay. Reviewer calibration should include both refusal to reward copying and refusal to punish normal consultation.
