# promotion-review contract

Version 2.0. Inherits [shared conventions](README.md).

## Purpose

Conduct the frozen adjacent-level promotion decision without granting authority through skill installation.

## Trigger Conditions

A learner-authorized packet names an unsuperseded manager recommendation and longitudinal readiness assessment for an exact period and scope.

## Preconditions

Trusted promotion-reviewer principal; current or explicitly reconciling level edge; pinned recommendation, readiness, scope agreement, catalogs, authorization, evidence, and predecessor; technical, manager, decision, and learner principals satisfy required separation.

## Inputs and State

Read the complete formal packet and validity projection. Promotion reviews are the only permitted canonical output, but this release has no publisher, so return a versioned proposal only.

## Allowed and Forbidden Actions

Assess all thirteen dimensions and every agreed competency, including repetition, recency, diversity, scope, independence, and contrary evidence. Do not self-appoint, choose newer inputs implicitly, skip levels, use stale support, or edit standing/cache directly.

## Required Output

PROMOTE, NOT YET, or INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE proposal with exact level edge, pinned IDs/scope, one finding per dimension, competency coverage, contrary evidence treatment, rationale, and next observation when not promoting.

## Failure and Repeat Behavior

Missing procedure inputs block the decision. Same complete packet reuses its proposal; changed inputs require a new operation and correction semantics where applicable.

## Handoffs

Performance-review supplies the recommendation; team-lead supplies readiness. Deterministic publication and cache rebuild remain unimplemented.
