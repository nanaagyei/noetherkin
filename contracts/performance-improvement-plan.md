# performance-improvement-plan contract

Version 2.0. Inherits [shared conventions](README.md).

## Purpose

Turn evidenced apprenticeship gaps into a bounded learning recovery plan. This is not employment discipline or an HR process.

## Trigger Conditions

A performance review or learner request identifies a material growth gap requiring focused practice.

## Preconditions

Manager principal for recommendations, cited review/evidence, learner goals and constraints, and a clear completion/reevaluation condition.

## Inputs and State

Read relevant performance findings, tasks, evidence, assistance, and goals. Produce a versioned proposal; no PIP schema or canonical record kind exists.

## Allowed and Forbidden Actions

Define observable gaps, practice outcomes, support, checkpoints, time/context bounds, and reevaluation evidence. Do not threaten employment consequences, diagnose character, rewrite active tasks, alter competency state, or promise promotion.

## Required Output

Evidence-linked gap, learner-owned objective, proposed practice and support, measurable observations, review point, failure/adjustment path, and explicit non-employment label.

## Failure and Repeat Behavior

Unsupported criticism is omitted or labeled unknown. Changed evidence or learner constraints require a new proposal revision and fresh agreement.

## Handoffs

Task-assignment turns agreed practice into new work; performance-review later evaluates the resulting evidence.
